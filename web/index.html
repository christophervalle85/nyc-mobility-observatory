    Continuous imagery

    The study footprint uses NYC TLC taxi zones. Map picks are approximate. Confirm the area in the search results.

    The TLC-derived borough hulls include water and approximate the study area.

    The land cutout uses generalized NYC DCP water-excluded borough land (26c). These simplified administrative boundaries approximate the shoreline.

    The scene uses Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles via Cesium ion. Imagery dates vary. Color and shadows come from the source imagery.
